Globex Industries — HR Policies

HIRING & ONBOARDING
All new hires complete a 90-day onboarding program with a assigned mentor.
Background checks are mandatory and must clear before the official start date.
New employees receive equipment and system access on day one.

REMOTE & HYBRID WORK
Globex operates a hybrid-first policy: a minimum of 2 in-office days per week.
Teams choose their in-office anchor days each quarter.
International remote work is limited to 20 working days per year for tax compliance.

PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
Formal performance reviews occur twice a year, in June and December.
Ratings range from 1 (needs improvement) to 5 (exceptional).
Promotions are considered during the December cycle only.

LEAVE POLICY
Employees accrue 22 days of annual leave, increasing by 1 day for every 2 years of service, up to 30.
Parental leave provides 16 weeks of fully paid time for the primary caregiver.
Bereavement leave provides up to 5 paid days.

GRIEVANCES
Concerns should first be raised with a direct manager.
Unresolved matters can be escalated confidentially to hr@globex.example.
