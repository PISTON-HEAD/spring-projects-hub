Globex Industries — Employee Benefits Guide

HEALTH INSURANCE
Globex covers 100% of the employee medical premium and 70% for dependents.
Dental and vision plans are optional add-ons at a subsidized rate.
Coverage begins on the first day of the month after your start date.

RETIREMENT (401k)
Globex matches employee 401k contributions dollar-for-dollar up to 6% of salary.
Employees are fully vested after 3 years of service.
You may change your contribution percentage once per quarter.

WELLNESS
Every employee receives a $600 annual wellness stipend for gym memberships or fitness equipment.
The Employee Assistance Program offers free, confidential counseling 24/7.
Two "recharge" mental-health days are provided each year, separate from annual leave.

LEARNING & DEVELOPMENT
Globex provides a $2,000 annual professional development budget per employee.
Certification exam fees are reimbursed once the certification is passed.
Conference attendance requires manager approval and a short trip report afterward.

PERKS
Commuter benefits cover up to $150 per month for public transport.
Employees receive a 25% discount on all Globex consumer products.
