Acme Corporation — IT Support FAQ

PASSWORD RESET
To reset your password, visit https://sso.acme.example/reset and follow the prompts.
Passwords must be at least 12 characters and include a number and a symbol.
Passwords expire every 90 days. You cannot reuse your last 5 passwords.

VPN ACCESS
Download the AcmeConnect VPN client from the internal portal under IT > Downloads.
Use your SSO credentials to sign in. The VPN is required for all remote access to internal systems.
If the VPN fails to connect, first check your internet, then restart the client.

SUPPORT HOTLINE
The IT support hotline is 1-800-ACME-HELP, staffed 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
For non-urgent issues, email helpdesk@acme.example; expect a response within one business day.
Critical outages should be reported by phone immediately.

HARDWARE REQUESTS
New laptops and peripherals are requested through the IT service portal.
Standard hardware requests are fulfilled within 3 business days.
Employees are entitled to a hardware refresh every 3 years.

SOFTWARE INSTALLATION
Approved software can be installed from the Acme Self-Service catalog without admin rights.
Requests for non-catalog software require manager and security team approval.
