Acme Corporation — Employee Handbook (2026 Edition)

WORKING HOURS
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM, Monday through Friday.
Core collaboration hours (when everyone must be reachable) are 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM.
Flexible start times between 7:00 AM and 10:00 AM are permitted with manager approval.

PAID TIME OFF
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave plus 10 public holidays.
Unused leave of up to 5 days may be carried over into the next calendar year.
Sick leave is capped at 12 paid days per year and requires a doctor's note after 3 consecutive days.

REMOTE WORK
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
Fully remote arrangements require director-level approval and a signed remote-work agreement.

CODE OF CONDUCT
Acme expects professionalism, respect, and integrity from every employee.
Harassment, discrimination, and conflicts of interest must be reported to People Operations.
Confidential company information must never be shared outside the organization.

EXPENSES
Business expenses under $200 can be approved by a direct manager.
Expenses of $200 or more require finance approval and an itemized receipt.
Reimbursements are processed on the 15th and last business day of each month.
